# MARS INVASION MODE

Completing M4 will unlock the MARS INVASION and a gun. MARS INVASION replaces 6 *regular stages* with special stages that randomly appear and rotate. MI8 will only be available after completing all other MARS INVASION stages. Only completed stages will be able to be played in the MARS INVASION MODE. MARS INVASION stages will appear every 30 minutes, randomly replacing an already completed stage. The replaced stage will not be able to have bots run on until the MARS INVASION is cleared or rotates. Other players bots will be hidden in the stage during MARS INVASION. 

These stages have MARS PEOPLE that chase the player, coming into contact with a MARS PEOPLE will result instant death. Players can use the gun to destroy MARS PEOPLE by clicking on MARS PEOPLE while holding the gun. It will automatically aim, then fire after a short delay. The player will be slowed down during the sequence. The gun has a cooldown of 2 seconds and takes 1 second to fire. Bullets travel instantaneously, requires line of sight, and kill in one shot.
